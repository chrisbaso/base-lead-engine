# HVAC Ops Pro Growth Goal

Build the highest-converting self-serve HVAC software affiliate funnel we can measure honestly.

Primary metric: qualified affiliate clicks per 100 quiz visitors.

Secondary metrics: quiz completion rate, first-email click rate, affiliate clicks by recommendation, and estimated conversions by variant.

Operating loop:

1. Keep three quiz-copy variants and three first-email variants active until each has enough traffic for a directional read.
2. Cut variants that bring partial leads but weak completed leads.
3. Shift paid traffic toward quiz copy that produces affiliate clicks, not just form starts.
4. Promote email angles that produce clicks without depressing open rate.
5. Use dashboard variant rows as the weekly decision surface before adding new creative.
