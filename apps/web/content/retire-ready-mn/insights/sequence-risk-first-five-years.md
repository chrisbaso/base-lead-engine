Sequence of returns risk is the risk that investment returns arrive in the wrong order. A retiree can earn a reasonable average return over thirty years and still run into trouble if the worst years happen near the beginning. The problem is withdrawals. Taking money from a falling portfolio can leave fewer dollars available to recover when markets improve.

The first five retirement years are especially important because the portfolio is often largest and withdrawals are just beginning. A bad sequence can permanently change the plan. This is why retirement income planning cannot rely only on average returns. Averages hide the path.

One defense is a cash reserve. Holding one to three years of planned withdrawals in cash or short-term holdings can reduce the need to sell long-term investments during a downturn. The right amount depends on spending flexibility, pension income, Social Security, and risk tolerance.

Another defense is a flexible withdrawal rule. A household may decide in advance to reduce discretionary withdrawals after a large market decline. This is easier to do before emotions are involved. Written rules can prevent panic decisions and keep both spouses aligned.

Dependable income can also help. Social Security, pensions, or other guaranteed income sources can cover essential expenses so the portfolio is not responsible for every dollar. The more fixed spending is covered by dependable income, the less pressure falls on investments in a weak market.

Taxes are part of the defense too. Selling from the wrong account during a downturn can create avoidable tax problems or reduce future flexibility. A withdrawal plan should identify which accounts are used first under normal conditions and which accounts are used during stress.

Questions worth asking before retirement include:

- How many years of spending are insulated from stock market declines?
- Which expenses can flex if markets fall?
- Which accounts fund withdrawals in a down year?
- What income continues regardless of markets?
- How will Social Security timing affect portfolio withdrawals?

Sequence risk cannot be eliminated, but it can be planned for. The best time to build the rules is before the first bad market of retirement.
